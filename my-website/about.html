About StudyHub AI

We're building the calm, focused study workspace we wished we had as students — made specifically for the way South African university students actually work.

Our mission

StudyHub AI turns scattered lecture notes, slides and textbooks into clear summaries, a manageable task list, and honest answers you can trust — all grounded in your own course material, with citations you can check.

We started StudyHub after watching friends at UCT and Wits juggle five different apps just to keep up with one semester. We built one calm workspace instead.

🎓

Built for SA students

Designed around real South African university terms, modules and academic calendars — not a generic template.

🔒

Your notes stay yours

Your uploaded files are never used to train our models by default. You're always in control of your data.

📈

Trusted by thousands

Over 8,000 students across UCT, Wits, UP and Stellenbosch already study smarter with StudyHub.
