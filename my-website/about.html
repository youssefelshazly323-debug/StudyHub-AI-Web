About StudyHub AI

We built StudyHub for students who are balancing classes, deadlines and a lot of information, and just want a simpler way to stay on top of it.

Our mission

StudyHub takes scattered lecture notes, slides and reading material and turns them into clearer summaries, more manageable tasks and practical study guidance. The idea is simple: help students stay on top of their work without the usual chaos.

We started from one problem a lot of students know too well — having to use five different apps just to manage one semester. We wanted something calmer, clearer and more realistic for everyday student life.

🎓

Built for South African students

Our tools are shaped around how students actually study, from planning each week to getting through assignments and revision.

🔒

Your notes stay yours

We keep your files private by default, and you stay in control of how they are used.

📈

Built to grow with you

Whether you are studying on your own or in a group, the platform is set up to match a normal semester workflow.
